## SLIDE 2: Problem Statement

### The Challenge
- Cabiao, Nueva Ecija has rich tourism potential but limited digital visibility
- Local businesses lack a unified platform to reach tourists
- Community engagement in environmental activities is fragmented
- No centralized way for visitors to discover Cabiao's attractions

### Our Solution
- A comprehensive digital tourism platform connecting tourists, businesses, and the community

## SLIDE 3: What is SMARTDCABIAO?

### Smart Tourism & Digital Cabiao

A **modern web application** that serves as the **official digital platform** for Cabiao, Nueva Ecija, Philippines

- 🌐 **Interactive Tourism Hub**
- 🏪 **Business Directory**
- 🎉 **Community Engagement**
- 🎁 **Rewards Program**

---

## SLIDE 4: Key Features Overview

### 1. Interactive Map
- Full-screen Leaflet-based map
- Filter by category (restaurants, markets, shops, landmarks, church)
- Click for details, directions, and contact info

### 2. Business Directory
- 8+ local businesses listed
- Searchable and filterable
- Business details: name, description, location, contact, hours, photos

### 3. Destinations Guide
- Tourist attractions and points of interest
- Rich descriptions and imagery
- Location and contact information

---

## SLIDE 5: Key Features Overview (Continued)

### 4. Favorites System
- Save your favorite places
- Persisted locally for quick access
- Create your personal Cabiao itinerary

### 5. Community Activities
- Browse local events (clean-ups, tree planting, festivals)
- Join and participate in community initiatives
- Calendar view of upcoming activities

### 6. Rewards Program
- Earn points for participating in activities
- Redeem vouchers at local businesses
- Incentivizes sustainable tourism
